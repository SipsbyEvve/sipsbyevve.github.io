💾 Backup to my computer

Downloads a copy of all your catalogue data — stickers, categories, orders, tumblers, promo, add-ons and photo settings — as one file you keep on your computer. Nothing on the site changes. (The photos themselves stay in Supabase; the file remembers which photo goes with each sticker.)

📦 Photos on GitHub

Puts copies of the sticker photos (small previews and the big 🔍 photos), covers, logo and the 4 big catalogue photos on GitHub, so customers browsing your catalogue use (almost) no Supabase traffic — even during lives. Your photos in Supabase stay exactly as they are. Do this after "Make photos lighter", and again every few months when you've added new stickers (new ones still show fine before that — they just come from Supabase).

🗜️ Make photos lighter

Makes a smaller copy of your big photos (stickers, category covers, catalogue photos), plus a small preview of each sticker, cover and the logo for the catalogue's lists — the full photo only loads when a customer taps 🔍. Each customer visit uses much less of your Supabase traffic, and the photos look the same. Nothing is deleted — your original files stay in Supabase Storage.

🥤 Tumblers

The tumbler styles customers can pick in step ①. Change a price, mark a style Sold Out (greyed out, can't be picked) or Preorder (can still be ordered, marked "Preorder"), or add a new style. Press Save tumblers when you're done.

📥 New 8-bottle lineup (01 470ml RM65 … 08 1200ml Glow RM109) — tap to fill it in below, check it, then press Save tumblers. Nothing changes for customers until you save.

Pattern prices for each bottle (e.g. Full Glitter +RM10 on the 03, not on the glow bottle) are in 🎨 Patterns below.

Tip: the tumbler photo in step ① is a separate picture — if you add a new style, also update "① Tumbler options photo" in the catalogue photos section.

🎨 Patterns

The patterns customers can pick in step ③. Set the extra price, or Hidden to keep a new one out of sight until you're ready. Special prices = a different price (or "not available") for one bottle, e.g. Full Glitter +RM10 on the 03. Press Save patterns when done.

Tip: also update "③ Patterns photo" in Catalogue photos when you add a new pattern.

🎁 Promotion

Buy a chosen tumbler (e.g. the 03 Fat Bottle), add a smaller bottle for a special price. Customers only see it while it's switched ON.

📈 Sales

🏷️ Suggested keywords

English keywords written from each sticker's picture, so customers can find them by what's on them ("sea turtle", "gold shells"…). Your own keywords are kept — only words that aren't there yet are added at the end. Nothing else changes (code, stock, status, photo stay the same).
